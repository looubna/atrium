How it works

Not a chatbot that gives advice: a place where you live the situation, make mistakes, and try again.

1

Choose a situation

A job interview built from your CV and the job, a pitch to investors, a thesis defence, small talk at the café, or a conversation in a new language.

2

Talk, out loud

The people in the room listen, answer and follow up in real time, with live voices and lip-synced faces, one-to-one or as a panel. Turn your camera on if you like: upper body when the scene is seated, full body when you stand to present.

3

Get feedback, then practise

See the exact moments where your answer, structure or delivery could be stronger, including your eye contact, gestures, posture and facial expressions when the camera is on, and jump straight to the exercise that trains it.
