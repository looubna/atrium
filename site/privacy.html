Atrium Privacy Policy & Terms

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Atrium is an AI coaching app: you practise real conversations (interviews, small talk, negotiations, exams and more) with a live AI voice, and get feedback afterwards. This page explains what we collect, why, who processes it, and your choices. Contact: hello@atriumcoach.com.

We ask for consent before any AI processing and before recording. You can withdraw it at any time in the app (Account), which stops new processing immediately. We don't sell your data, and the app contains no advertising or tracking SDKs. In the hackathon build, Atrium sends the resume text and role details needed for written interview preparation to Nebius Token Factory for NVIDIA Nemotron inference. This integration does not send recordings, transcripts or camera video to Nebius; those continue to use Google Cloud AI. AI providers process data to provide Atrium's selected features under their applicable service terms. See Nebius Token Factory subprocessors and Nebius's privacy policy.

Your choices

  • Download or delete recordings from the app.
  • Delete your account in the app (Account → Delete account): your data is removed; backups expire within 7 days.
  • Ask us anything about your data at the contact address above.

Plans and payments

  • Free trial: 20 live minutes, once per person: one per phone, and not for accounts made with throwaway email services.
  • Pro ($49.99/month): 10 hours of live practice a month, up to 1 hour a day. Pro Max ($89.99/month): 20 hours a month, up to 2 hours a day. Prices may vary by country; the store shows your price before you pay.
  • Subscriptions renew monthly until cancelled in Google Play › Profile › Payments & subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current month stays active. Unused minutes don't carry over. Refunds follow the store's policy.
  • Promo codes unlock a plan for a limited time and can be used once.

Terms of use

  • Atrium is for practice and coaching. Feedback is generated by AI and can be wrong; it is not professional, medical, legal or psychological advice.
  • Don't use Atrium to harass, impersonate or harm anyone, or to upload content you have no right to share.
  • You must be at least 16, or have a parent's or guardian's permission.
  • We may change these terms; the app asks you to accept important changes again.
