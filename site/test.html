Help launch Atrium on Google Play

Atrium lets you practise interviews, presentations and everyday conversations out loud, with live AI people and detailed feedback. To go live on Google Play, it needs testers. It takes two taps on your Android phone.

1Join the testers

Opens Google Groups. Sign in with the Gmail you use on Google Play, then tap Join group. If you only see “You don't have permission”, open this page in Chrome instead of WhatsApp or Instagram.

Join the testers

2Install the app

Tap Become a tester, then install Atrium Coach from Google Play.

Install Atrium

Thank you! One small favour

Please keep the app installed for 14 days and try a room or two. New accounts get 20 free live minutes. Ideas or problems? Write to hello@atriumcoach.com.

If Google Play says the app isn't available, wait a few minutes after step 1 and try again. On iPhone or a computer? Use Atrium in your browser at app.atriumcoach.com.
